WaCash privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Who is responsible

WaCash is a personal finance app for Android created by Wascar Pujols (the "developer"). For any privacy question, write to pujolsdev13@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • Your transactions, categories, tags, attachments and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • WaCash has no servers of its own, no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The developer does not receive, see or sell your data, and does not share it with third parties.
  • The only time the app connects to the internet is if you turn on the Google Drive backup.

3. What data the app handles

DataWhere it stays
Transactions (amount, date, description), categories, tags, budgets and recurring itemsThe app's private database, on your phone
Attachments (photos and PDFs you choose)The app's private internal storage

4. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on the Drive backup, WaCash creates a file with your database and attachments and encrypts it on your phone (AES-256-GCM) with a password only you know. It then uploads it to a hidden app folder inside your own Google Drive, using only the drive.appdata permission. The developer has no access to that folder or to your account. The last 10 versions are kept. Google handles data stored in Drive under its own privacy policy.

Important: if you lose the backup password, nobody can open the backup, including the developer.

You can also create and restore a manual backup (an encrypted .wacash file) and export your transactions to CSV, Excel or PDF. Those files are saved wherever you choose with the system file picker.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13 or later): only if you turn on the daily reminder, a recurring item with confirmation, or budget alerts. It is optional.
  • Internet: only for the Google Drive backup, always over HTTPS.
  • Fingerprint or face: only to unlock the app if you turn it on.
  • Camera, photos and files: WaCash does not request these permissions. It uses the system camera, the photo picker and the document picker, and only receives what you choose.

6. Retention and deletion of your data

  • On your phone: you can delete transactions in the app (they stay in the trash for 30 days, then are erased) or uninstall WaCash, which removes all local data.
  • In Google Drive: under More › Backup › Disconnect you can choose to also delete all WaCash backups from your Drive. You can also revoke the permission at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Because the developer does not receive your data, there are no copies to delete.

7. Security

  • Optional lock with PIN and fingerprint or face; while the app is locked, your information is not visible inside it. The home screen widget, if you choose to add it, shows the month's cash flow.
  • An option to hide WaCash in screenshots and in the recent apps view.
  • The app does not allow Android to copy its data into automatic backups: only WaCash's encrypted backups apply.
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS.

8. Children

WaCash is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way WaCash handles data changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
